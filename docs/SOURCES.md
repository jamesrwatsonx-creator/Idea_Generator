# Research source catalog

This is a discovery registry, not a claim that every source is connected, reusable, or commercially validated. NoSignups is prioritized.


## Market

| Source | Purpose | Access / status | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [AlternativeTo](https://alternativeto.net/) | Replacement candidates and community software recommendations. | browser / registered_not_connected | Recommendations do not establish actual switching or spending. |
| [Toolify](https://www.toolify.ai/) | AI product categories and directory-reported visibility. | browser / registered_not_connected | Traffic and directory prominence are not retention or profit; sponsored placements exist. |
| [Public Canny boards](https://feedback.canny.io/) | Find public requests and comments; discover boards specific to target products. | browser / registered_not_connected | Votes are self-selected; this URL is Canny product feedback, not a global request dataset. |
| [CFPB Consumer Complaint Database](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/consumer-complaints/) | Research financial-product complaint categories through documented downloads/API. | official-api / registered_not_connected | Not representative; normalize carefully and inspect current narrative availability. |
| [Google Trends](https://trends.google.com/) | Search-interest context, geography, vocabulary and seasonality. | browser / registered_not_connected | Relative search interest is not absolute demand or willingness to pay. |
| [Similarweb](https://www.similarweb.com/) | Research digital traffic and acquisition signals within available access. | browser / registered_not_connected | Metrics may be estimates; programmatic access and coverage depend on plan. |
| [TrustMRR](https://trustmrr.com/docs/api) | Permitted market research using documented startup revenue fields. | official-api / registered_not_connected | API policy prohibits business cloning, bulk republication and other uses. Review acceptable use before connector activation. |
| [G2](https://www.g2.com/) | Software reviews and alternatives for a selected category. | browser / registered_not_connected | Retrieval timed out; no review dataset acquired. Self-selection and vendor incentives require scrutiny. |
| [Capterra](https://www.capterra.com/) | Software category, pricing and review research. | browser / registered_not_connected | Retrieval unsupported in this session; inspect current access terms and original review evidence. |
| [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/) | Ask/Show discussions, workarounds and technical product references. | browser / registered_not_connected | Developer-heavy self-selected sample; votes are not purchasing evidence. |
| [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps) | App descriptions, visible ratings and selected product review research. | browser / registered_not_connected | Store rankings and related suggestions do not establish actual co-installation; inspect country/date/access. |
| [Apple App Store](https://apps.apple.com/) | Product listings, pricing disclosures and app-specific review research. | browser / registered_not_connected | Storefront and geography affect results; no actual co-purchase dataset inferred. |
| [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/) | Discover launches, product categories and discussion leads. | browser / registered_not_connected | Launch attention, promotion and upvotes do not establish demand or retention. |
| [Reddit practitioner communities](https://www.reddit.com/) | Find specific practitioners describing workarounds and failed alternatives. | browser / registered_not_connected | Use relevant public communities with allowed access; anecdotes are self-selected and APIs have separate terms. |
